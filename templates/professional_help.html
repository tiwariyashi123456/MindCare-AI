Take care of your mental well-being

If stress, anxiety, sadness or other difficulties are affecting your daily life, talking to a qualified professional can provide useful support and guidance.

🧠

Mental Health Professionals

Different professionals can provide different types of support.

🧑‍⚕️

Psychologist

A psychologist can help you understand thoughts, emotions and behaviors and can provide counseling and psychological support.

👩‍⚕️

Psychiatrist

A psychiatrist is a medical doctor who can evaluate mental health conditions and provide medical treatment when appropriate.

💬

Counselor / Therapist

Counseling and therapy can help you work through emotional difficulties, stress, relationships and everyday challenges.

💡

When Should You Seek Help?

Consider talking to a professional if difficulties are becoming hard to manage.

😟

You feel persistently sad, worried or overwhelmed.

😴

Your sleep or daily routine is being affected.

📚

Your studies, work or relationships are becoming difficult to manage.

🧠

You are experiencing thoughts or feelings that are difficult to handle on your own.

🌱

Things You Can Try

Small healthy habits can support your overall well-being.

🧘 Meditation Take a few quiet minutes to relax and focus.
📖 Journaling Write down your thoughts and feelings.
🏃 Exercise Regular physical activity can support well-being.
👨‍👩‍👧 Talk to Someone Share your feelings with someone you trust.
🚨

Need Immediate Help?

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services or go to the nearest emergency department. You can also contact a trusted person and ask them to stay with you.

You deserve immediate support when you need it.

MindCare AI is a wellness-support application and does not replace professional medical care.
