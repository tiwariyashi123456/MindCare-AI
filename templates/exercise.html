Light Walking

A simple walking activity to refresh your body, improve movement and give your mind a short break.

⏱️ 5 Minutes 🚶 Easy

💡 About

Walk slowly and comfortably while paying attention to your breathing and surroundings.

📋 How to do it

  1. Stand comfortably and relax your shoulders.
  2. Start walking at a slow and comfortable pace.
  3. Keep your breathing natural and relaxed.
  4. Keep your posture comfortable while walking.
  5. Continue for about 5 minutes.
  6. Slow down gradually before stopping.
✨ Why try it?

Gentle movement can help you take a mental break and stay physically active.

Gentle Stretching

Gentle stretches that can help release body tension and make you feel more relaxed.

⏱️ 5 Minutes 🤸 Easy

💡 About

Use slow and comfortable movements. Never force your body into a painful position.

📋 How to do it

  1. Stand or sit comfortably.
  2. Relax your shoulders and neck.
  3. Slowly move one body part at a time.
  4. Hold a gentle stretch without bouncing.
  5. Breathe slowly throughout the movement.
  6. Release the stretch slowly and relax.
✨ Why try it?

Gentle stretching can help you relax and become more aware of body tension.

Relaxation Exercise

A calm activity designed to help you slow down, relax your body and focus your attention.

⏱️ 10 Minutes 🧘 Calm

💡 About

Find a comfortable position and give yourself a few quiet minutes without rushing.

📋 How to do it

  1. Sit or lie down in a comfortable position.
  2. Close your eyes if that feels comfortable.
  3. Relax your shoulders, hands and face.
  4. Take slow and comfortable breaths.
  5. Notice areas of tension and gently relax them.
  6. Continue calmly for about 10 minutes.
✨ Why try it?

Relaxation exercises can provide a quiet break and help you focus on the present moment.

Breathing Exercise

Slow and comfortable breathing to help you calm your body and focus your attention.

⏱️ 2 Minutes 🌬️ Calm

💡 About

Focus on slow, comfortable breathing. Do not force your breath or hold it uncomfortably.

📋 How to do it

  1. Sit comfortably with your back relaxed.
  2. Relax your shoulders.
  3. Take a slow breath in through your nose.
  4. Slowly breathe out in a comfortable way.
  5. Continue this gentle breathing pattern.
  6. Stop if you feel dizzy or uncomfortable.
✨ Why try it?

Slow breathing can provide a simple moment of calm and help you focus your attention.
